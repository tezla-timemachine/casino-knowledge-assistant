# Annual Leave Policy (2024)

*Harbourlight Casino Group. Mock document for a portfolio project. Not a real company policy.*

## 1. Purpose

This policy sets out employees' paid annual leave entitlement and the rules for booking and carrying forward leave.

## 2. Scope

This policy applies to all permanent employees in Harbourlight venues and offices.

## 3. Leave year

The leave year runs from 1 January to 31 December.

## 4. Entitlement

Full-time employees receive 28 days of paid annual leave each leave year, including bank holidays. After 5 years' continuous service, employees receive 1 extra day (29 days in total). Part-time employees receive a pro-rata entitlement.

## 5. Carrying leave forward

Employees may carry forward up to 5 days of unused annual leave into the next leave year. Carried-forward days must be used by 31 March of the new leave year, or they are lost.

To carry forward leave, employees must ask their line manager before 1 December.

## 6. Booking leave

Leave is requested through StaffHub with at least 2 weeks' notice, whatever the length of the leave. Leave between 20 December and 2 January is not normally approved for gaming floor employees.

## 7. Exceptions

Exceptions to the carry-forward rules for long-term sickness or family leave must be approved by the Head of HR.

## 8. Leaving the company

Accrued but untaken leave is paid in the final salary. Leave taken in excess of entitlement is deducted.

# Expenses Policy

*Harbourlight Casino Group. Mock document for a portfolio project. Not a real company policy.*

## 1. Purpose

This policy explains which work-related costs can be claimed and how to claim them.

## 2. Making a claim

Claims are made in ExpenseHub within 30 days of the cost being incurred, with an itemised receipt for every item. Claims are approved by the line manager and paid in the next monthly payroll after approval. Claims made more than 90 days late will not be paid.

## 3. Travel

- Use standard-class rail, booked in advance where possible.
- Mileage in a personal car is paid at 45p per mile. Normal commuting to your usual workplace cannot be claimed.
- Taxis may be claimed only when public transport is not available, or between midnight and 06:00 after a late shift.

## 4. Hotels

Hotel stays must be booked through the company travel portal. The maximum room rate is £150 a night in London and £100 a night elsewhere.

## 5. Meals

When staying away overnight on company business, meals may be claimed up to £25 a day. Alcohol cannot be claimed.

## 6. What cannot be claimed

Personal items, fines (including parking fines), alcohol, gambling, and training course fees (these are paid directly by the company under the Training and Development Policy) cannot be claimed.

## 7. Company cards

Company credit cards are issued only to employees who travel regularly, with Finance Director approval.

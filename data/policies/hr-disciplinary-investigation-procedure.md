# Disciplinary Investigation Procedure

*Harbourlight Casino Group. Mock document for a portfolio project. Not a real company policy.*

**Restricted: HR team and investigating managers only.**

## 1. Purpose

This procedure guides HR and managers through a fair investigation before any disciplinary hearing.

## 2. Appointing an investigating officer

HR appoints an investigating officer who is not in the employee's direct reporting line and has had no involvement in the matter.

## 3. Suspension

Suspension is used only where there is a risk to people, evidence or the investigation, or in cases of possible gross misconduct. Suspension is on full pay, is not a disciplinary sanction, and is reviewed every 2 weeks.

## 4. Gathering evidence

The investigating officer interviews the employee and any witnesses, and collects relevant documents, system records and CCTV through the Head of Security. Investigations should normally be completed within 4 weeks.

## 5. Investigation report

The report sets out the facts found and recommends whether there is a case to answer. It does not decide the outcome.

## 6. Hearing

If there is a case to answer, the employee receives written notice at least 48 hours before the hearing, with copies of the evidence. The employee has the right to be accompanied by a colleague or trade union representative.

## 7. Outcomes and appeal

Possible outcomes are no action, a first written warning, a final written warning, or dismissal. The employee may appeal in writing within 5 working days of receiving the outcome.

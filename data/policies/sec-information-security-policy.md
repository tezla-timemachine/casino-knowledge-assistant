# Information Security Policy

*Harbourlight Casino Group. Mock document for a portfolio project. Not a real company policy.*

## 1. Purpose

This policy protects customer, employee and company information from loss, theft and misuse.

## 2. Information classification

All information is classified as one of the following:

| Class | Examples | Rules |
|---|---|---|
| Public | Marketing material, published opening hours | Can be shared freely |
| Internal | Rotas, internal announcements | Employees only |
| Confidential | Customer details, payroll data, contracts | Need to know only; encrypt if sent outside the company |
| Restricted | CCTV footage, security plans, investigation files | Named people only; never sent by email |

## 3. Access

Access to systems is given on a need-to-know basis and approved by the system owner. Access is removed on the employee's last day, or when they change role.

## 4. Passwords and sign-in

- Passwords must be at least 14 characters long.
- Multi-factor authentication (MFA) must be used for every system that supports it.
- Passwords must never be shared, written down or reused across systems.
- Screens must be locked whenever a device is left unattended.

## 5. Lost or stolen devices

A lost or stolen company device must be reported to the IT Service Desk immediately, and in any case within 1 hour of noticing. Reporting quickly allows the device to be wiped remotely.

## 6. Phishing

Suspicious emails must be reported using the Report Phish button in Outlook. Do not click links or open attachments in a suspicious email. If you have already clicked a link or entered your password, report it to the IT Service Desk immediately.

## 7. CCTV

CCTV footage is restricted information. Only the Security team may view, copy or release footage. Requests for footage, including from the police, must go to the Head of Security.

## 8. Personal data breaches

Any suspected personal data breach must be reported to the IT Service Desk within 1 hour. The Data Protection Officer decides whether the breach must be reported to the Information Commissioner's Office, which must happen within 72 hours where required.

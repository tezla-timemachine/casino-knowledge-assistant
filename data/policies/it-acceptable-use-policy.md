# IT Acceptable Use Policy

*Harbourlight Casino Group. Mock document for a portfolio project. Not a real company policy.*

## 1. Purpose

This policy explains how employees may use company computers, phones, email and internet access.

## 2. Company devices

Company devices are provided for business use. Limited personal use, such as checking the news on a break, is allowed if it does not interfere with work and does not break this policy.

Employees must not:

- install software that has not been approved by IT;
- connect personal USB drives or other storage devices;
- disable antivirus or security updates;
- let anyone else use their company device or account.

## 3. Personal devices

Personal devices must not be used to store or process company or customer information. Company email may be read on a personal phone only through the approved Outlook app with MFA turned on.

## 4. Email

Company email must not be automatically forwarded to personal email accounts. Confidential information must only be sent outside the company in line with the Information Security Policy.

## 5. AI tools

Only the AI tools listed as approved on the IT intranet may be used for work. Customer information, employee information and confidential company information must never be entered into a public or personal AI tool.

## 6. Internet use

Employees must not access gambling, adult, illegal or hateful content on company devices or networks. Access to some websites is blocked.

## 7. Passwords

Employees must follow the password and sign-in rules in section 4 of the Information Security Policy.

## 8. Monitoring

The company monitors the use of its devices, email and networks to protect security and to check compliance with this policy. Monitoring is carried out in line with data protection law.

## 9. Getting help

Contact the IT Service Desk through StaffHub or on extension 4357 for IT help, access requests or to report a problem.

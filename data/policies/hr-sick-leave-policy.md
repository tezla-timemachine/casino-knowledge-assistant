# Sickness Absence Policy

*Harbourlight Casino Group. Mock document for a portfolio project. Not a real company policy.*

## 1. Purpose

This policy explains what to do if you are too ill to work, how sick pay works and how we support employees back to work.

## 2. Reporting sickness

If you are too ill to work, phone your line manager or the duty manager. Do not text or email unless you cannot speak.

- Office-based employees must call at least 1 hour before their usual start time.
- Gaming floor, security and cage employees must call at least 2 hours before the start of their shift, so that cover can be arranged.

You must call again each day you are absent unless your manager agrees otherwise, or you have a fit note.

## 3. Evidence of sickness

For absences of up to 7 calendar days, you self-certify by completing the return-to-work form in StaffHub on your first day back.

For absences of more than 7 calendar days, you must provide a fit note from a doctor, nurse, pharmacist or physiotherapist covering the 8th day onwards.

## 4. Sick pay

Statutory Sick Pay (SSP) is paid to eligible employees in line with current government rules.

After completing probation, employees also receive company sick pay, which tops up SSP to full basic pay, based on length of service, in any rolling 12-month period:

| Length of service | Company sick pay |
|---|---|
| Less than 1 year | SSP only |
| 1 to 3 years | 2 weeks at full pay |
| 3 to 5 years | 4 weeks at full pay |
| More than 5 years | 8 weeks at full pay |

## 5. Return to work

Your manager will hold a short return-to-work conversation with you within your first 2 shifts back. The purpose is to check you are fit to work and to agree any support you need.

## 6. Attendance review

An attendance review meeting is arranged if you have either:

- 3 separate periods of sickness absence in 6 months; or
- 10 or more days of sickness absence in 12 months.

The review is supportive. It may lead to a referral to Occupational Health.

## 7. Sickness during annual leave

If you are ill during booked annual leave and provide a fit note covering the days of sickness, those days are treated as sickness absence and the annual leave is returned to you. You must report the sickness on the first day, following section 2.

## 8. Support

The Employee Assistance Programme offers free, confidential support 24 hours a day. Details are in the Employee Handbook.

# Customer Complaints Procedure

*Harbourlight Casino Group. Mock document for a portfolio project. Not a real company policy.*

## 1. Purpose

This procedure explains how to handle customer complaints fairly and quickly, as required by our Gambling Commission licence.

## 2. Resolving on the spot

Listen to the customer, apologise where appropriate, and try to resolve the complaint immediately if you can. If you cannot, call the duty manager.

## 3. Logging complaints

Every complaint that is not resolved on the spot must be logged in CaseHub before the end of the shift, with the customer's contact details and a description of the complaint.

## 4. Disputes about a game

If a customer disputes the outcome of a game or a payout, the duty manager must request a CCTV review from Security. The table or machine is not reopened until the duty manager agrees.

## 5. Timescales

| Step | Deadline |
|---|---|
| Acknowledge the complaint | Within 2 working days |
| Send a final response | Within 8 weeks |

## 6. Independent review

If the customer is not satisfied with our final response, they can refer the complaint to our independent Alternative Dispute Resolution (ADR) provider. The final response letter must include the ADR provider's details.

## 7. Abusive customers

Employees do not have to accept abuse. If a customer becomes abusive or threatening, call Security and follow the Incident Reporting Procedure.

# Incident Reporting Procedure

*Harbourlight Casino Group. Mock document for a portfolio project. Not a real company policy.*

## 1. Purpose

This procedure explains how to report incidents so that people are kept safe, problems are dealt with quickly, and we meet our legal and regulatory duties.

## 2. Emergencies

If anyone is in immediate danger, call 999 first. Then alert Security Control on radio channel 1, or by calling extension 2222.

## 3. What to report and when

| Type of incident | Examples | How to report | Deadline |
|---|---|---|---|
| Security | Theft, violence, threats, suspected cheating | Tell the duty manager, then log in SafeLog | Before the end of your shift |
| Health and safety | Injuries, near misses, spills, fire alarms | Tell the duty manager, then log in SafeLog | Before the end of your shift |
| IT and data | Lost device, phishing click, data sent to the wrong person | IT Service Desk | Within 1 hour |
| Safer gambling | A customer showing signs of gambling harm, a self-excluded customer on site | Tell the duty manager immediately, then log in SafeLog | Before the end of your shift |
| Underage person | Someone who appears under 18 on the gaming floor | Ask for ID (Think 25) and tell the duty manager immediately | Log before the end of your shift |

## 4. Suspected money laundering

Do **not** report suspected money laundering in SafeLog, and do not discuss it with the customer or with colleagues who do not need to know. Telling a customer that they are under suspicion ("tipping off") is a criminal offence.

Report the suspicion directly to the Money Laundering Reporting Officer (MLRO) using the internal suspicious activity report form, as described in the Compliance Guidelines.

## 5. What to include in a report

Include the date, time and exact location; what happened, in your own words; the people involved and any witnesses; and any action taken. Do not guess or give opinions.

## 6. After you report

The duty manager reviews every SafeLog report before the end of the shift. Serious injuries that must be reported to the Health and Safety Executive are reported by the Health and Safety Manager. You may be asked to give a written statement.

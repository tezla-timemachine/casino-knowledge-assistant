# Employee Onboarding Guide

*Harbourlight Casino Group. Mock document for a portfolio project. Not a real company policy.*

## 1. Before your first day

Before you start, HR will:

- check your right to work in the UK;
- carry out a basic criminal record (DBS) check;
- confirm whether your role needs a Personal Management Licence (PML) from the Gambling Commission. Roles in specified management positions need one, and you cannot start those duties until it is granted. HR will tell you if this applies to you;
- send your contract and your StaffHub login details.

## 2. Your first day

On your first day you will:

- collect your photo ID badge from Security. The badge must be worn at all times on site;
- have your uniform fitted, if your role has one;
- receive your company laptop or locker, depending on your role;
- meet your buddy, an experienced colleague who will help you through your first month.

## 3. Your first two weeks

You must complete the mandatory induction training listed in the Training Policy within your first 2 weeks. Gaming floor employees cannot work unsupervised on the gaming floor until it is complete.

## 4. Probation

All new employees have a 6-month probation period. Your manager will hold review meetings at 3 months and 6 months. Probation may be extended by up to 3 months if more time is needed.

During probation, the notice period on either side is 1 week.

## 5. Systems you will use

| System | What it is for |
|---|---|
| StaffHub | Rotas, leave requests, payslips, sickness forms and HR help |
| LearnHub | Training courses and training records |
| SafeLog | Reporting incidents and accidents |
| IT Service Desk | IT help, lost devices and access requests |
| ExpenseHub | Expense claims |

## 6. Who to contact

Your line manager is your first contact. Your buddy can help with day-to-day questions. HR can be contacted through the HR Help form on StaffHub.

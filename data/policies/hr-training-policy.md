# Training and Development Policy

*Harbourlight Casino Group. Mock document for a portfolio project. Not a real company policy.*

## 1. Purpose

This policy sets out the training every employee must complete, and the support available for further learning.

## 2. Mandatory training

| Course | Who | When |
|---|---|---|
| Safer gambling and social responsibility | All employees | At induction, then every year |
| Anti-money laundering (AML) and counter-terrorist financing | All employees | At induction, then every year |
| Information security awareness | All employees | At induction, then every year |
| Health, safety and fire awareness | All employees | At induction, then every 2 years |
| Age verification (Think 25) | Venue employees | At induction, then every year |
| First aid at work | Designated first aiders | Every 3 years |

All courses are completed in LearnHub, which keeps each employee's training record.

## 3. Deadlines

Induction training must be completed within the first 2 weeks of employment. Yearly refresher courses must be completed by the anniversary of the previous completion.

If mandatory training is more than 30 days overdue, the employee will not be placed on gaming floor rotas, and office-based employees will lose access to customer systems, until the training is complete.

## 4. Professional development

Employees who have passed probation can apply for study support for a qualification relevant to their role. Study support covers:

- course fees of up to £1,000 per year; and
- up to 5 days of paid study leave per year for exams and study.

Applications are made through LearnHub and approved by the line manager and the Head of Learning and Development.

## 5. Repaying study support

If an employee leaves the company within 12 months of a course finishing, they must repay 100% of the course fees. If they leave between 12 and 24 months after the course finishes, they must repay 50%.

## 6. Training expenses

Travel and accommodation for approved training are claimed under the Expenses Policy. Course fees are paid directly by the company and must not be claimed as expenses.

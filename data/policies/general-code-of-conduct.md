# Code of Conduct

*Harbourlight Casino Group. Mock document for a portfolio project. Not a real company policy.*

## 1. Who this applies to

This Code applies to all employees, contractors and agency workers. Breaking the Code may lead to disciplinary action, up to and including dismissal.

## 2. Gambling by employees

Employees must not gamble at any Harbourlight venue or on any Harbourlight online product, whether on or off duty. This rule continues for 30 days after an employee leaves the company.

Employees who gamble elsewhere must not let it affect their work. Any employee who is worried about their own gambling can get confidential help through the Employee Assistance Programme without it affecting their job.

## 3. Tips

Gaming employees must never keep tips personally. All tips given to gaming employees are placed in the tronc box at the table and shared through the tronc scheme. Hospitality employees follow their venue's tipping process.

## 4. Gifts and hospitality

- Employees must not accept any gift from a customer worth more than £25.
- Every gift or hospitality from a supplier, of any value, must be recorded in the Gifts and Hospitality Register within 5 working days.
- Any gift or hospitality from a supplier worth more than £50 needs Compliance approval before it is accepted.
- Cash or cash equivalents, such as vouchers or chips, must never be accepted from suppliers.

## 5. Conflicts of interest

Employees must declare to HR any close personal relationship with a colleague in their reporting line, and any personal relationship with a customer they serve regularly. Employees must not deal with transactions involving family members or friends.

## 6. Alcohol and drugs

Employees must not work under the influence of alcohol or drugs. Drinking alcohol on shift, including on breaks, is not allowed. Random testing may be carried out in safety-critical roles.

## 7. Social media

Employees must not post about customers, customers' wins or losses, or security arrangements, and must not post photos taken on the gaming floor or in back-of-house areas.

## 8. Confidentiality

Customer information, business information and security information must be kept confidential during and after employment.

## 9. Speaking up

Employees who suspect wrongdoing, such as fraud, cheating, money laundering or a breach of this Code, should raise it with their manager or Compliance. Concerns can also be raised anonymously through the confidential Speak Up line, which is run by an independent provider and available 24 hours a day. Employees who raise concerns in good faith will not suffer any detriment.

# Hybrid Working Policy (2023)

*Harbourlight Casino Group. Mock document for a portfolio project. Not a real company policy.*

## 1. Who can work from home

Employees in office-based support roles may work from home for up to 3 days a week. Customer-facing roles in venues cannot work from home.

## 2. Core hours

Employees working from home must be available between 10:00 and 15:00.

## 3. Equipment

Employees may use their own computer to work from home if it has up-to-date antivirus software.

## 4. Working abroad

Working from outside the UK is not permitted.

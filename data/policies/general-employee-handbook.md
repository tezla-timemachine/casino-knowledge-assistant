# Employee Handbook

*Harbourlight Casino Group. Mock document for a portfolio project. Not a real company policy.*

## 1. Welcome

Welcome to Harbourlight Casino Group. We run casinos, restaurants and bars in four UK venues, supported by our head office. This handbook gives an overview of working here. Detailed rules are in the policies it refers to, and where they differ, the detailed policy applies.

## 2. Our values

Play fair, look after people, and keep it safe. Every employee is expected to follow the Code of Conduct.

## 3. Working hours and rotas

Venues are open 24 hours a day. Venue employees work shifts according to rotas, which are published in StaffHub 4 weeks in advance. Office employees usually work 37.5 hours a week, Monday to Friday.

## 4. Breaks

Employees working more than 6 hours are entitled to a 20-minute paid break. Shifts of more than 8 hours include a 45-minute break in total. Breaks are scheduled by the duty manager on gaming floors.

## 5. Pay

Employees are paid monthly on the last working day of the month. Payslips are available in StaffHub. Night shifts between midnight and 06:00 are paid at an enhanced rate of time and a quarter.

## 6. Holidays

Full-time employees receive 28 days of paid holiday a year, including bank holidays, with extra days for long service. The rules for booking and carrying forward holiday are in the Annual Leave Policy.

## 7. Uniform and appearance

Venue employees are given a uniform, which must be clean and worn only at work. Name badges must be visible at all times.

## 8. Personal phones

Personal phones must not be carried on the gaming floor or in the cage. They must be kept in your locker during your shift and can be used on breaks in staff areas.

## 9. Staff benefits

- Free meals in the staff restaurant on every shift.
- 25% discount in Harbourlight restaurants and bars when off duty. The discount does not apply to gaming.
- Employee Assistance Programme: free, confidential advice and counselling, 24 hours a day, by phone or online.
- Cycle-to-work scheme.

## 10. Staff and gambling

Employees must not gamble at any Harbourlight venue or on any Harbourlight online product. The full rules are in the Code of Conduct.

## 11. Getting help

Your line manager is your first point of contact. HR can be reached through the HR Help form on StaffHub. Concerns about wrongdoing can be raised confidentially as described in the Code of Conduct.

# Compliance Guidelines for Employees

*Harbourlight Casino Group. Mock document for a portfolio project. Not a real company policy.*

## 1. Why compliance matters

Harbourlight is licensed by the Gambling Commission. Our licence depends on keeping crime out of gambling, being fair and open with customers, and protecting children and vulnerable people. Every employee has a part to play.

## 2. Customer due diligence

Customers must be identified and verified before they buy chips, exchange cash, or make transactions totalling €2,000 or more (or the sterling equivalent) within 24 hours. Cage employees follow the identification checklist in the cage procedures.

If a customer refuses to provide identification, the transaction must not go ahead and the duty manager must be told.

## 3. Spotting suspicious activity

Signs that may suggest money laundering include:

- buying chips with large amounts of cash and cashing out with little or no play;
- asking for winnings to be paid to someone else, or in a cheque;
- splitting transactions to stay below the €2,000 threshold;
- gambling far more than the customer appears able to afford.

## 4. Reporting a suspicion

If you suspect money laundering, complete the internal suspicious activity report form and send it directly to the Money Laundering Reporting Officer (MLRO) on the same day. Do not investigate yourself and do not tell the customer. Tipping off is a criminal offence.

## 5. Safer gambling

Employees must watch for signs of gambling harm, such as long sessions without breaks, chasing losses, borrowing money, or visible distress. If you see these signs, tell the duty manager, who will decide on a customer interaction.

## 6. Self-exclusion

A customer who has self-excluded must not be allowed to gamble. If you recognise a self-excluded customer on site, tell the duty manager immediately. Do not challenge the customer yourself.

## 7. Age verification

Nobody under 18 may enter the gaming floor. We operate Think 25: anyone who appears to be under 25 must show valid photo ID.

## 8. Further help

Questions about these guidelines go to the Compliance team. Compliance procedures used by the Compliance team itself are not published to all employees.

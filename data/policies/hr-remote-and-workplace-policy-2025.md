# Remote and Workplace Policy

*Harbourlight Casino Group. Mock document for a portfolio project. Not a real company policy.*

This policy replaces the Hybrid Working Policy (2023).

## 1. Purpose

This policy explains which roles can work remotely, how often, and the rules for working in our offices and venues.

## 2. Venue-based roles

Gaming floor, cage, security, hospitality and customer service roles in venues are performed on site. These roles cannot be done remotely.

## 3. Office-based support roles

Employees in office-based support roles (for example Finance, HR, IT, Compliance and Marketing) may work remotely for up to 2 days a week, with their line manager's agreement.

Tuesday and Wednesday are anchor days. All office-based employees are expected to be in the office on anchor days unless they are on leave or have agreed otherwise with their manager.

There are no set core hours. Employees agree their working pattern with their team, but must be reachable during their team's agreed working hours.

## 4. Home working allowance

Employees whose contract says they work remotely at least 1 day a week receive a home working allowance of £26 a month, paid through payroll.

## 5. Equipment and security

Company work must be done on a company laptop. Personal computers, tablets and phones must not be used to store or process company or customer information. See the IT Acceptable Use Policy.

When working remotely, employees must use the company VPN and must not work on confidential information in public places where screens can be overlooked.

## 6. Working abroad

Employees in office-based support roles may work from outside the UK for up to 10 working days in a calendar year. This must be approved in advance by the line manager and by the Information Security team, because of the risk to company devices and data. Requests are made through the IT Service Desk at least 2 weeks before travel.

## 7. Office workplace rules

Offices use hot desking. Desks are booked in StaffHub. A clear desk rule applies: at the end of the day, no papers or devices may be left on desks, and confidential papers must be locked away or placed in the confidential waste bins.

## 8. Requests to work differently

Employees who want a different working arrangement, for example compressed hours, can make a flexible working request through StaffHub. Requests are answered within 2 months.

# Annual Leave Policy (2026)

*Harbourlight Casino Group. Mock document for a portfolio project. Not a real company policy.*

## 1. Purpose

This policy explains how much paid annual leave employees receive, how to book it and what happens to leave that is not used by the end of the leave year.

## 2. Scope

This policy applies to all permanent employees, full-time and part-time, in every Harbourlight venue and support office. Casual and zero-hours workers are covered by section 9.

## 3. Leave year

The leave year runs from 1 January to 31 December.

## 4. Entitlement

Full-time employees receive 28 days of paid annual leave each leave year, including bank holidays. Because the casino is open on bank holidays, bank holidays are not automatically taken as leave; they are part of the 28 days and are booked like any other day.

Long-service days are added as follows:

- After 3 years' continuous service: 1 extra day (29 days in total).
- After 5 years' continuous service: 2 extra days (30 days in total).

The maximum entitlement is 30 days. Part-time employees receive a pro-rata entitlement based on their contracted hours.

## 5. Carrying leave forward

Employees may carry forward up to 8 days of unused annual leave into the next leave year. Carried-forward days must be used by 30 June of the new leave year. Any carried-forward days not used by 30 June are lost and are not paid.

Employees do not need approval to carry forward up to 8 days. The days carried forward are shown automatically in StaffHub from 2 January.

## 6. Booking leave

Leave is requested through StaffHub. Employees must give notice of at least twice the length of the leave requested. For example, a request for 5 days of leave must be made at least 10 days before the first day of leave.

Gaming floor and security rotas are published 4 weeks in advance. Requests made after a rota is published can only be approved if cover is available.

Managers should respond to a request within 5 working days.

## 7. Peak periods

The following are peak periods: 24 December to 1 January, and the weekends of major sporting events listed on the intranet each year. During peak periods, gaming floor and security employees may take no more than 5 consecutive days of leave, and only with approval from the General Manager of their venue.

## 8. Exceptions to the carry-forward limit

An employee may carry forward more than 8 days only if they could not take their leave because of:

- long-term sickness;
- maternity, paternity, adoption or shared parental leave; or
- business reasons, where their manager cancelled approved leave and the Head of People has approved the exception in writing.

In these cases up to 20 days may be carried forward. Days carried forward because of sickness must be used within 18 months of the end of the leave year in which they were earned. Other exceptional carry-forward must be used by 30 June, as in section 5.

## 9. Casual and zero-hours workers

Casual and zero-hours workers accrue holiday at 12.07% of the hours they work. Accrued holiday is shown in StaffHub and can be booked in the same way as other leave.

## 10. Leaving the company

When an employee leaves, any leave they have accrued but not taken in the current leave year is paid in their final salary. If they have taken more leave than they have accrued, the difference is deducted from their final salary.

## 11. Questions

Questions about this policy should go to your line manager or the HR team through the HR Help form on StaffHub.
